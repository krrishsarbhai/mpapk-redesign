Our History & Mission

The journey of Jagrut Apang Sanghatana since 1981

A Humble Beginning

The present JAGRUT APANG SANGHATANA was initially called THE MAHATMA PHULE APANG PRASHIKSHAN KENDRA. The Kendra was established in 1981, THE INTERNATIONAL YEAR OF THE DISABLED.

The Jagrut Apang Sanghatana had a very humble beginning when a small enterprising group of young orthopedically handicapped persons came together with a desire to do something constructive for other young orthopedically handicapped persons.

As their first venture, they decided to start a small Vocational Training Programme in Tailoring just for a few orthopedically handicapped persons. They thus rented a small two-room tenement for this purpose and collected a group of ten orthopedically young persons, interested in the Training. Thus started the first Training Program of the Sanghatana.

This small experiment of theirs proved so successful that it whetted their appetite still further to do something much more constructive for the handicapped and to a much larger scale. They also desired to provide residential facilities for the trainees in view of the normal mobility problems faced by orthopedically handicapped persons.

Expansion Since 1990

Initially, the "Sanghatana" did not have premises of its own for running the planned 'Residential vocational training programs'. Hence it rented a small place at Karve Road, Pune for this purpose and started two, one-year Training Courses namely, one in "Television and Radio-Audio Repairs" and other in "Cutting and Tailoring". However, due to constraint of space the "Kendra" at that time could admit only a total number of twenty candidates for both the courses per year.

By June 1995, the Kendra had trained a total number of 325 candidates in the above two trades.

In June 1995, the sanghatana was lucky enough to acquire a one-acre plot of land of its own, at 'Maan', village near Pune. The sanghatana immediately got spacious premises of its own constructed with residential hostel accommodation available for 40 trainees per year. Now, every year the sanghatana admits a total 40 Handicapped trainees, on residential basis. During selection process of the trainees, preference is given to the Rural Handicapped youth usually to those who come from the socio-economically underprivileged state of society.

Aims and Objectives of the Organization

  • To expand diversity and add some more vocational facilities to the present set-up suitable for the Handicapped.
  • To start Vocational Training Programmes specially suitable for the rural setting and rural needs, in view of the large number presently taking advantage of the present facilities.
  • To set up a small Prosthetic and Orthotic repairs workshop, so as to enable the trainees to get minor repairs and modifications done to their appliances right on the premises itself.
  • To assist the Trainees in getting their periodical medical evaluation done and if necessary, arrange for corrective surgery or modifications to their appliances.
  • To start hostel facilities for handicapped ladies.
  • To start an Awareness programme for the general public regarding the Environmental Barriers faced by the handicapped in relation to their access to public utility and Recreation facilities. For example, at present hardly any Cinema House or Drama Theatre has a ramp or railing which could make them accessible for the handicapped.

Ways in Which You Can Help

  1. Sponsor a trainee for his/her entire training programme.
  2. Help the organization by donating food grains etc. either on a monthly or yearly basis.
  3. Donate bedsheets, utensils etc.
  4. Undertake to pay Monthly electricity or telephone bills, buy necessary stationery for the organization etc.
  5. Offer to give work related to tailoring etc. to the trainees.
  6. Undertake to construct a room in the building. The sponsor's name will be given to the room.
  7. Give scholarship or outright sponsorship to any of the trainees.
