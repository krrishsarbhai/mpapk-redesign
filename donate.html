Donate & Support Us

This is how you can help the specials in particular and the society in general.

Ways You Can Contribute

  • Tax Exemption: Donations in cash are exempted from Income Tax under section 80(G).
  • Infrastructure: Donate generously for the development of Physiotherapy room, purchase of new well-equipped bus, multipurpose hall, Updating the computer lab, old Computers and Covering the swimming pool with a folding shade.
  • Empowerment: Providing employment, Vocational Training and organize funds for equipment and training of persons with disability to start a business for financial independence.
  • Volunteer Time: Spending some time with the specials is one of the most important ways you can support. Be a volunteer for the various activities and events.
  • Sponsorships: Sponsor various events, rehabilitation, cultural and training programs. Donation in kind such as Material for educational use, Toys, Sports Articles, Cupboards etc.
  • Corporate Support: Donation through CSR programs or voluntary work in the institution through CSR programs.

This is a reasoned appeal to your generosity. We hope therefore you will rise to the need of the hour and take effective steps to set right the sorry state of specials.
