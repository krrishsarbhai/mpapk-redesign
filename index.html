Who We Are

Jagrut Apang Sanghatana

Established in 1981, we are an organization driven entirely by people with disabilities. We are dedicated to promoting the welfare and social integration of rural handicapped youth through free, industry-recognized vocational training.

Facilities & Post-Training Assistance

Recognized by the Maharashtra Technical Board, Govt. of Maharashtra, we provide a one-year free hostel and mess facility. Also, we are providing them free necessary stationery and equipment, uniforms, and medical facilities. We enclose herewith our brochure about our activities for your kind perusal and are hoping for your visit to our campus at your convenience.

Every year our organization is contributing towards the development of passed trainees by giving them equipment like sewing machines, toolkits, tricycles, etc., to make them self-sufficient. Since our inception, we have nurtured nearly 800 physically disabled trainees in our institute and made them self-sufficient in society, and we pledge the same ideology prevails in the future also.
